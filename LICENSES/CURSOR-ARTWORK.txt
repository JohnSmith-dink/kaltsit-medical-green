The maintainer confirmed that the cursor artwork included in this theme is
the artwork represented by the kaltsit-cursors project. This release keeps
the Kaltsit-Sihengtu installation identifier and uses the upstream multi-size
Xcursor files pinned at 7ef139a6210fd8eb03d0296b624d71eaa3120589.
All 17 binaries and 68 upstream aliases are unchanged; 8 legacy compatibility
aliases are retained separately. Sizes: 24, 32, 48, 64, 96 pixels.
See docs/cursor-source.json and the bundled upstream build-report.json.
